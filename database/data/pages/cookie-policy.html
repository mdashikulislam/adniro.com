Cookie policy

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. This page explains which cookies Adniro uses and how you can control them.

Necessary cookies

These cookies make the site work, so they cannot be switched off. They do not track you across other websites.

  • Session cookie: keeps you signed in and remembers what you did on the current visit, such as a listing you are writing.
  • Security cookie (XSRF-TOKEN): protects forms against requests sent by other websites.
  • Preference cookies: remember your language, country and display choices.
  • Cookie choice (site_cookie_consent): remembers whether you accepted analytics cookies, for one year.

Analytics cookies (only with your permission)

If you choose "Accept all", we use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, for example which pages are visited and how people find us. Google Analytics sets cookies such as _ga and _ga_*, kept for up to two years. We use this information only in summary form to improve the site.

If you choose "Necessary only", Google Analytics is not loaded at all.

Changing your choice

Use the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page to change your choice at any time. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will then ask you again.
