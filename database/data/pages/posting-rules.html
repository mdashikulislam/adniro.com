Posting rules

These rules keep Adniro useful, fair and safe. Listings that break them are not published, or are removed. Repeated or serious breaches can lead to the account being closed.

Every listing must

  • Be for one real item, property, job or service that you can actually sell, rent or provide. Post other items separately.
  • Be in the right category and city.
  • Describe it honestly, including its condition and any faults.
  • Use your own photos of the actual item. No stock pictures, logos or text-only images.
  • Show a real price, or leave the price empty. No fake prices to appear higher in searches.
  • Follow the law where you and the buyer live.

Not allowed

  • Illegal drugs, prescription medicine and drug equipment.
  • Weapons, ammunition, explosives and parts that make them work.
  • Stolen goods, counterfeit or replica items, and fake documents, IDs or certificates.
  • Adult or sexual content and services.
  • Anything that puts children at risk.
  • Human trafficking, and jobs that ask applicants to pay to start.
  • Protected wild animals and products made from them.
  • Hazardous or regulated goods sold without the required licence.
  • Hate, violence or extremist content.
  • Scams: deposits for items you do not have, "too good to be true" offers, requests for codes or payment outside the meeting.
  • Spam: the same listing posted several times, listings that only advertise another website, and misleading titles.

How listings are checked

New and edited listings can be checked automatically before they go live. When a listing is unclear, a person on our team reviews it. If a listing is not approved, we email you the reason, and you can edit it and try again.

See something wrong?

Use the Report abuse link on the listing. If you think a decision about your own listing was wrong, contact us.
