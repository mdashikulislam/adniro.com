A simple, safe place to buy and sell locally

Adniro is a free classifieds marketplace. People use it to sell things they no longer need, find good second-hand deals, rent out a place, offer a service or find work close to home.

What you can do here

  • Post a listing for free. Describe your item, add photos and choose your city. The AI assistant can fill in the form for you from one description.
  • Search and browse by category and location, and save the listings and searches you care about.
  • Talk to buyers and sellers with our built-in messages, without sharing your email address.
  • Follow sellers you like and see their new listings.

How we keep it safe

  • Sellers who confirmed their email address show a Verified seller badge.
  • New listings can be checked automatically before they go live, and anything unclear goes to a person on our team.
  • Every listing has a Report abuse link, and we act on reports.
  • Our safety tips explain how to meet, pay and spot common scams.

Our rules

To keep the marketplace useful and fair, every listing must follow our posting rules. Read how we handle your data in our privacy policy.
