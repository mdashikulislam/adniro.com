AI Features

We use artificial intelligence (AI) services from trusted providers, such as Anthropic, for two features:

  • Listing checks: the text and photos of a new or edited listing may be sent to the AI service to check that it follows our posting rules. Listings the AI cannot clearly approve are reviewed by a person on our team, and you are told the reason if a listing is not approved.
  • The AI assistant in the listing form: when you use it, the description you type or record is sent to the AI service so it can fill in the form. Phone numbers and email addresses are removed from what it writes back.
  • Voice input: when you speak instead of typing, the recording is either turned into text by your browser's own speech service (in Chrome, this is processed by Google) or sent to our speech-to-text provider. We do not keep the recording after it has been turned into text.

We send only what the feature needs, we do not use AI to make decisions about you beyond whether a listing can be published, and we do not sell this data.
