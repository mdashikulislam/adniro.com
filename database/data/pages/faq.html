Frequently asked questions

Posting a listing

How do I post a listing?
Click "Create Listing" at the top of any page. Choose a category, add a title, a description, a price and photos, then choose your city. You can post without an account if the site allows it, but with an account you can manage and renew your listings.

What does it cost?
Posting a listing is free.

Can the AI fill in the form for me?
Yes. At the top of the listing form, describe what you are selling in your own words, by typing or by speaking. The AI assistant picks the category and writes the title, description, price, details and city. You check and change everything before you continue. The box shows how many AI posts you have left; when they run out, you can still fill in the form yourself.

How many photos can I add?
Up to 5 photos per listing. Clear photos of the actual item sell faster.

What can't I sell?
Read our posting rules. In short: nothing illegal, dangerous, stolen, fake or misleading.

After posting

Why isn't my listing online yet?
Listings can be checked before they go live. Most are checked within minutes. If a person needs to look at it, it can take longer. You can see the status of your listings in your account.

My listing was not approved. What now?
We email you the reason. Edit the listing to follow the posting rules and it will be checked again. If you think we got it wrong, contact us.

How long does my listing stay online?
A listing stays online for 30 days. It is then archived, and you can put it back online from your account within the next 7 days before it is removed.

I sold my item. How do I remove the listing?
Sign in, open "My listings" in your account, and delete or archive it.

Buying and contacting sellers

Will people see my email address?
No. Buyers contact you through our messages, and your email address stays private. Your phone number is shown only if you add it to your listing.

What does "Verified seller" mean?
The seller confirmed their email address. It is a good sign, but always follow our safety tips before you pay.

How do I report a listing?
Use the "Report abuse" link on the listing. We review every report.

Your account and data

How do I delete my account?
Sign in and choose "Close account" in your account menu. Read our privacy policy to see what we keep and for how long.

How do I stop marketing emails?
Open "Preferences" in your account, or use the "Email preferences" link at the bottom of our emails.
